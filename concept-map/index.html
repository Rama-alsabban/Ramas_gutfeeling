Centre

Identity is the only word on both lists, so it is the origin. Both axes and every contrast line pass through it.

Left ↔ right · Time

Left looks back to what was inherited. Right works in the present. Time is drawn as the axis itself.

Down ↔ up · scale

Down is one life, Personal. Up is a society, Socio-political approach. The higher a concept sits, the more it faces a public.

Distance from centre

Three rings move from why to what. Lens: how the work sees. Method: how it is made. Form: what the audience meets.

On an axis

A concept on an axis bridges two halves. Contrast, Bilingual, Visual narratives and Interdisciplinary sit there.

Opposite sides

Pairs that pull against each other sit diametrically, so the line between them crosses Identity. Contrast is the rule that draws them.
